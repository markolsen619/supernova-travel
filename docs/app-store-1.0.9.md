# App Store text for 1.0.9

> Drafted 2026-10-31 for iOS build 58 (Android: version code 14). Changes from 1.0.8: What's New; DESCRIPTION adds Home, past trips from photos, invite links and playback highlights, and moves "plan together" out of Pro (inviting is free; a shared budget is Pro); review notes swap NEW IN 1.0.8 for NEW IN 1.0.9 (invite links and the one-time Allow Paste prompt explained) and FREE VS PRO updated.
> **App Privacy: no change.** Invite links store the trip, the inviter and who used the link (app functionality, linked to the user, like trip invites today); the clipboard is read once on a brand-new account only to find an invite link and is never stored or sent; past-trip photos are uploaded like any journal photo (Photos or Videos, already declared) with their metadata removed, and their places are named by Mapbox (already listed).

## What's New

A new Home with today's plan, what's coming up and ideas for your next trip. Invite anyone to a trip by text or email, even if they don't have Supernova yet, and inviting is now free. AI itineraries keep each day in one part of town, in walking order, and Nova now tells you about the history, phrases and little-known facts of where you're going. Long trips play back city by city with each day's photos. On iPhone, add a trip you've already taken straight from your photos. Feed videos can now be up to a minute.

## App Review notes

```
ABOUT THIS APP
A trip planner for independent travelers: it drafts a day-by-day itinerary from a destination and dates, lets you edit it, keeps bookings and boarding passes in one wallet, and lets you share trips. We do not sell travel.

DEMO ACCOUNT
Use the email and password in the Sign-In Information fields above. Profile > Trips holds an AI-generated 7-day Lisbon itinerary. You can also generate a fresh one from the Create tab; this month's free AI trip is available.

FREE VS PRO
Free accounts can plan and share trips, invite friends to them, use the maps, browse destinations, post, search and message. They get one AI itinerary a month (one city), 10 Ask Nova messages a month and up to two wallet items entered by hand. Pro adds unlimited and multi-city AI itineraries, 300 Nova messages a month, a shared trip budget on trips with others, an unlimited wallet with AI import from booking confirmations, and live flight alerts. The demo account is free and holds five wallet items from before the two-item limit; adding another opens the paywall. Purchasing Pro in the sandbox unlocks everything immediately.

NEW IN 1.0.9
Invite links: a trip > Invite > Invite with a link shares a single-use link; whoever opens it joins the trip after signing up. Installing from it, the web page copies the link before the App Store opens, and a brand-new account checks the clipboard once to find it, so iOS may ask "Allow Paste" one time; nothing is stored or sent. Past trips: Create > Add a trip you've taken (photo picker, no library permission). Also the Home tab and city-by-city playback of long trips.

SIGN-IN OPTIONS
Email/password, Google and Sign in with Apple. Please use the demo account.

IN-APP PURCHASES
Supernova Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription (each with a 1-week free trial for new subscribers) or a one-time lifetime purchase, all granting the same entitlement. Offer codes redeem via "Redeem a code" on the paywall or in Settings. To reach the paywall: Settings > Subscription, or on a free account add a third wallet item or generate a second AI trip in a month. "Restore purchases" is on both paywalls and in Settings > Subscription.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (1.2)
Posts, comments, trips, place reviews, profiles and DMs are user-generated. Each has a three-dot menu with Report and Block; authors can delete their own posts, and any comment on their own posts. Reported content is hidden from the reporter immediately, and content reported by three distinct users is hidden from everyone pending review. A text filter rejects slurs and sexual content before anything is saved. Our terms (community guidelines, zero tolerance) are at https://supernova-a2125.web.app/terms, linked from the welcome screen, the paywall and Settings.

ACCOUNT DELETION (5.1.1(v))
Settings > Account > Delete account deletes the account and its content in the app. For Sign in with Apple accounts we also revoke the Apple token.

AI AND THIRD-PARTY DATA (5.1.2(i))
Itinerary generation, Ask Nova and booking-confirmation parsing send trip details (for Nova also the user's message and their bookings on that trip, never confirmation codes) to Google Gemini via our server, only after explicit in-app consent. The demo account has already consented (Settings > Privacy withdraws it).

AGE RATING
13+. Under-13 sign-ups are blocked by a date of birth check; the date is not stored.

LOCATION
Never requested.

SUPPORT
support@galaxielabs.space — https://supernova-a2125.web.app/support

EXTERNAL SERVICES
Firebase, Google Gemini (server-side, consent required), Google Places, Mapbox, Algolia, RevenueCat (over Apple IAP), AviationStack, Apple Wallet push, Expo push, Cloudflare (email import), Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In. All in the privacy policy. No ads or analytics.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None.

REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not regulated: a planning tool, not a travel agency.
```
