## Description

```
Supernova is where you plan the trip and where you keep it.

Tell it where you're going, when, and how you like to travel. It builds a day-by-day itinerary — places to eat, things to see, and time to do nothing. Then change all of it.

HOME
• Today's plan while you're traveling, with the weather, your next trip with a countdown, and what's coming up
• On flight day: a check-in reminder, when to leave for the airport, and your flight live on the Lock Screen (Pro)

DISCOVER
• Browse 89 destinations by region and vibe — beaches, food, culture, nightlife and more
• Each destination has itineraries from the Supernova team and other travelers, labeled by style, plus the places they keep coming back to
• Spin the globe to see where travelers are heading, what's trending, and which friends are on a trip right now

SAVE IT FOR LATER
• Share a TikTok or up to five screenshots to Supernova and the places in them go to your wish list
• Saved places show up when you plan a trip to that city, and on your trip in the right city

PLAN
• Generate a full itinerary with AI from a destination, your dates and a travel style — trips of up to three weeks, with each day kept in one part of town
• Ask Nova, your trip planner, to change any part of a trip, or ask about the place: its history, a useful phrase, tipping, little-known facts. You see every change before it happens
• Build trips by hand instead, day by day, or make any trip you can see your own as a starting point
• Add a trip you've already taken from your photos, with its places and days worked out for you
• Plan multi-city trips city by city: set the nights in each, reorder them, and open one city at a time
• Weather for each day, tickets and tours for the sights on your trip, and directions to any stop in Maps
• See every stop on a 3D map with landmarks and terrain, or in satellite view, and play your trip back as a calm flyover
• Your trips and boarding passes open without a connection, so they're there on the plane

TRAVEL TOGETHER
• Invite friends to a trip from Supernova, or send a link by text or email to anyone — they join the trip when they sign up
• Plan the same itinerary and packing list together, and see each other's bookings on the trip

YOUR TRAVEL WALLET
• Boarding passes with a scannable code, so you're not digging through email at the gate
• Add a boarding pass to Apple Wallet; when its gate or time changes, the pass in Wallet updates too
• Subscribe to your trips in Apple Calendar or Google Calendar: trip days, flights, stays and reservations, kept up to date
• Hotels, cars, restaurants, trains, buses and ferries in one list, grouped by trip
• Forward confirmation emails to your own Supernova address and they land in your wallet, matched to your trip (Pro)

SHARE
• Your Year in Travel: trips, days away, cities, kilometres and your best photos as story slides
• Share any trip as an image for your Stories
• Post photos and video clips of up to a minute
• Follow travelers and save the trips you want to take
• Make a trip public, followers-only, or keep it to yourself; keep your account private and approve who follows you

SUPERNOVA PRO
Free accounts get one AI itinerary a month, 10 messages with Nova a month and two wallet items. Supernova Pro adds unlimited AI itineraries, including multi-city trips across several countries; much more time with Nova; a shared trip budget to split costs with your group; an unlimited wallet with bookings imported from your confirmation emails; and live flight alerts, a time to leave for the airport and a Live Activity on flight day.

Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in your App Store account settings. Deleting your Supernova account does not cancel a subscription.

Terms of use: https://supernova-a2125.web.app/terms
Privacy policy: https://supernova-a2125.web.app/privacy
```

## App Review notes

```
ABOUT THIS APP
A trip planner for independent travelers: it drafts a day-by-day itinerary from a destination and dates, lets you edit it, keeps bookings and boarding passes in one wallet, and lets you share trips. We do not sell travel.

DEMO ACCOUNT
Use the email and password in the Sign-In Information fields above. Profile > Trips holds an AI-generated Lisbon itinerary, a Paris trip and a multi-city Europe trip. You can also generate a fresh one from the Create tab; this month's free AI trip is available.

FREE VS PRO
Free accounts can plan and share trips, invite friends, use the maps, browse destinations, save a wish list, post, search and message. They get one AI itinerary a month (one city), 10 Ask Nova messages a month, 20 wish-list reads a month and up to two wallet items entered by hand. Pro adds unlimited and multi-city AI itineraries, 300 Nova messages a month, a shared trip budget, an unlimited wallet with AI import from booking confirmations, live flight alerts, a leave-for-the-airport time and a flight Live Activity. The demo account is free; adding a wallet item opens the paywall. A sandbox purchase unlocks Pro at once.

NEW IN 1.1.0
Wish list: share a TikTok link or up to five screenshots to Supernova from the share sheet (or Profile > Trips > Wish list > Add screenshots); after AI consent, the caption or images are read on our server by Gemini to find the places, and the person confirms them before anything is saved (privately). Year in Travel: Profile > your year. Tours & tickets: activity stops link to GetYourGuide or Viator search pages (affiliate links to third-party services; nothing is sold in the app). Flight day: a check-in reminder and, for Pro, a Live Activity. Weather from MET Norway. The app may ask for a rating at most once every 120 days, through Apple's standard prompt.

SIGN-IN OPTIONS
Email/password, Google and Sign in with Apple. Please use the demo account.

IN-APP PURCHASES
Supernova Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription (each with a 1-week free trial for new subscribers) or a one-time lifetime purchase, all granting the same entitlement. Offer codes redeem via "Redeem a code" on the paywall or in Settings. To reach the paywall: Settings > Subscription, or on a free account add a third wallet item or generate a second AI trip in a month. "Restore purchases" is on both paywalls and in Settings > Subscription.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (1.2)
Posts, comments, trips, reviews, profiles and DMs each have Report and Block in their three-dot menu. Reported content is hidden from the reporter at once, and from everyone after three distinct reports, pending review. A text filter rejects slurs and sexual content before saving. Terms (community guidelines, zero tolerance): https://supernova-a2125.web.app/terms

ACCOUNT DELETION (5.1.1(v))
Settings > Account > Delete account deletes the account and its content in the app. For Sign in with Apple accounts we also revoke the Apple token.

AI AND THIRD-PARTY DATA (5.1.2(i))
Itinerary generation, Ask Nova, the wish list and booking import send what they need (trip details; Nova's message and the user's bookings on that trip, never codes; a post's caption or the user's screenshots) to Google Gemini via our server, only after explicit in-app consent. The demo account has already consented (Settings > Privacy withdraws it).

AGE RATING
13+. Under-13 sign-ups are blocked by a date of birth check; the date is not stored.

LOCATION
Never requested.

SUPPORT
support@galaxielabs.space — https://supernova-a2125.web.app/support

EXTERNAL SERVICES
Firebase, Gemini (server-side, with consent), Google Places, Mapbox, MET Norway, Algolia, RevenueCat, AviationStack, APNs, Expo push, Cloudflare, GetYourGuide/Viator (links only), Apple and Google sign-in. All in the privacy policy. No ads or analytics.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None.

REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not regulated: a planning tool, not a travel agency.
```
