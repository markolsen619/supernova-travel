Privacy policy

Supernova is a travel app for planning trips, sharing travel posts, and keeping your bookings in one place. This policy explains what we collect, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have.

Supernova is operated by Galaxie Labs ("we", "us"). Questions go to support@galaxielabs.space.

The short version: we collect what you give us to run the app. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, full name, and username. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the name and email address that provider shares.
  • Date of birth: asked for at sign-up only to confirm you're 13 or older. We don't store it.
  • Profile: an optional photo, bio, and location you type in.
  • What you post and plan: posts, photos, videos, captions, place tags, comments, likes, follows, trips and itineraries, trip journal entries, trip budgets and expenses, trip invitations, and direct messages.
  • Travel wallet: boarding passes (airline, flight number, times, seat, gate, and barcode data), reservations (including confirmation codes), and loyalty programs (member numbers and balances).
  • Booking imports: the text of any confirmation you paste in so we can add it to your wallet.
  • Forwarded emails (Supernova Pro): if you create an email import address, the emails you send or forward to it. We read each one to find bookings, add them to your wallet, and keep a short log of what arrived (the subject line, when, and what was added). We don't keep the email itself.
  • AI trip requests: the destination, dates, travel style, and preferences you enter.
  • Place reviews: the rating, words and photos you add to a place. If the place is on one of your trips, your review shows a "Visited on a trip" badge; we work that out from your trips but don't show which trip.
  • Reports and blocks: the reason and any details you give when you report content or an account, and the accounts you block. The person you report or block isn't told who you are.

Information collected when you use the app

  • Push notification token, so we can send notifications you've allowed.
  • Subscription status: whether you have Supernova Pro, when it renews or expires, and your purchase history. Payment is handled by Apple or Google; we never see your card details.
  • Usage counts needed to apply free-plan limits, such as how many AI trips you've generated this week.
  • Map diagnostics: when part of a trip map fails to load, a short record of the failure (which map layer, the error message, the trip, your app version, iPhone model and iOS version), tied to your account, so we can fix it. At most a few per map you open.
  • Technical information such as IP address and device type, which the services below receive when the app connects to them.

Camera, photos, and location

Supernova uses your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo or video. It doesn't read your device's GPS location. Places attached to posts and trips are the ones you search for or choose, with one exception you start yourself: when you add a trip you've taken from your photos, the app reads when and where each photo you picked was taken, on your phone, to build the trip's days and stops. To name the places, those positions are sent to our map provider, Mapbox; they are not stored by Supernova. The photos are uploaded without their location, and each stop is saved at the place's own position (or its neighbourhood's), never where a photo was taken. Supernova's assistant receives the place names and times to suggest a title and captions, never your photos or their locations. On Android this uses the "access photo locations" permission.

How we use it

  • To run your account and show your profile, posts, and trips to the people you've chosen to share them with.
  • To generate AI itineraries and turn pasted or forwarded confirmations into wallet entries, and to match bookings to your trips.
  • To check the status of flights in your wallet and, for Pro members, send flight alerts.
  • To power search, your feed, messages, and notifications.
  • To provide Supernova Pro and apply free-plan limits.
  • To show friends on a trip: during trips you share with followers or everyone, people who follow you can see your profile photo at that day's city on the globe. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy.
  • To show what's trending: we count, anonymously, how many trips people start for each destination, and show busier places as warmer areas on the globe. Places appear only once several trips have been started there, and only in broad bands, never as counts. No one can see who is planning a trip.
  • To keep Supernova safe: filtering objectionable language before it's posted, reviewing reports, hiding content that several people report, and removing content and accounts that break our terms of use.

Who can see your information

Other people on Supernova. Your name, username, profile photo, bio, and follower counts are visible to other signed-in users. Posts, place reviews (with their photos and any "Visited on a trip" badge), and trips you make public, are visible to others. When anyone shares a link to a public trip by a public account, its title, cover photo, first few stops, and your name can be seen on the web by whoever opens the link; links to other trips show no trip details. Trips set to followers-only or private are limited to those audiences. Your travel wallet and saved trips are visible only to you. Direct messages are visible to the people in the conversation.

Service providers. We use these companies to run Supernova. Each gets only what it needs to do its job:

  • Google Firebase stores your account, content, and files, runs our servers, and handles sign-in.
  • Google Gemini API processes AI trip requests and pasted or forwarded booking confirmations to produce itineraries and wallet entries. We ask your permission before sending anything, and nothing is sent unless you use an AI feature. You can withdraw permission in Settings → Privacy → AI data sharing.
  • Ask Nova, the trip planner: when you ask Nova for changes to a trip, we send that trip’s cities, days and stops, your message and the recent messages in that conversation, and the name, date and time of the trip’s bookings, including the bookings in your wallet that fall on this trip, to Google’s Gemini API. We never send confirmation codes, addresses or boarding-pass barcodes. If you tell Nova about a booking, it can add it to your wallet once you accept. Nothing on your trip changes unless you accept it. Your conversation is stored privately in your account and is deleted with the trip or your account.
  • Google Places receives place searches and returns place details and photos.
  • Mapbox provides maps and receives technical information when they load.
  • Algolia powers search and holds a copy of public profiles and public trips.
  • RevenueCat manages subscriptions and receives purchase records linked to your account ID.
  • Apple and Google process payments and, if you use them, sign-in.
  • AviationStack receives flight numbers and dates to look up flight status.
  • Expo delivers push notifications.
  • Cloudflare receives email sent to your email import address and passes it to our servers. It doesn't keep the email.

Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety and rights of our users and of Supernova.

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, content, wallet, and messages you've sent, and remove your public profile and trips from search. Copies in backups and service provider systems are removed on their normal schedules. Purchase records may be kept where the law requires it, and reports you made or that were made about you may be kept for as long as we need them to keep Supernova safe and meet legal obligations.

Your trips and wallet items are also kept on your device so you can see them without a connection. That copy stays inside the app, refreshes when you're online, and is removed when you sign out or delete your account.

If you turn on the calendar link, your trips and booking times are available at a private address you add to a calendar app. You can make a new link or turn it off at any time in Settings.

If you add a boarding pass to Apple Wallet, Wallet gives us an identifier for your device's Wallet and a notification token, so we can update that pass when its gate, time or status changes. We keep them only while the pass is in your wallet, and delete them with the pass or your account.

If you add a boarding pass to Google Wallet, its flight details (route, times, seat, gate) and the pass's barcode — which your airline encodes with your name and booking reference — are sent to Google so the pass can appear in your Google Wallet. We update it there when those details change, and remove the barcode and details if you delete the pass or your account.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile, posts, trips, and wallet entries at any time.
  • Control visibility of each trip from its edit screen.
  • Block or report anyone from the three-dot menu on their content or profile, and review who you've blocked in Settings → Privacy.
  • Turn off notifications in your device settings.
  • Delete your account in the app under Settings → Account → Delete account, or by emailing support@galaxielabs.space.
  • Access or correct your data: email us and we'll help. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to a copy of your data. We'll honor those requests and won't treat you differently for making them.

A subscription is managed through your Apple or Google account. Deleting your Supernova account doesn't cancel it, so cancel it there first.

Children

Supernova isn't for children under 13, and sign-up requires confirming you're at least 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data travelling between the app and our servers is encrypted, and access rules limit private information such as your wallet to your own account. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Where your data is processed

Supernova's servers are in the United States, and our service providers may process data in other countries. By using Supernova, your information may be transferred to and processed in those places.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the date at the top and let you know in the app.
