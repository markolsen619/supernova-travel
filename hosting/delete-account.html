Delete your account

You can delete your Supernova account and its data at any time, from inside the app or by asking us.

In the app

  • Open Supernova and sign in.
  • Go to Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email support@galaxielabs.space from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Supernova account”. We confirm it's you and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, photo, posts and videos, comments, likes, follows, messages, place reviews and photos.
  • Your trips and itineraries, and your travel wallet: boarding passes, reservations, loyalty programs and email-import address.
  • Your notifications, saved trips, and your place on friends' globes.

What we keep

  • Anonymous totals that can't be linked back to you, such as how many trips were planned to a city.
  • If you told us why you cancelled Pro, that note is kept without your name.
  • Records we must keep by law, such as purchase receipts held by Apple or Google.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Supernova Pro subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions first.
